# Business-Intelligence-Dashboard
# 📊 Business Intelligence Dashboard

## Overview

This project presents an interactive **Business Intelligence Dashboard** developed in **Power BI** using the **Sample Superstore** dataset. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of business performance through executive, customer, product, and profit analysis, enabling data-driven decision-making.

---

## Business Objective

The dashboard aims to:

- Monitor overall business performance.
- Analyze sales and profitability across product categories.
- Evaluate customer purchasing behavior.
- Identify top-performing products and regions.
- Discover opportunities to improve profitability.

---

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Power Query
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Modeling

---

## Dashboard Pages

### Executive Overview
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Profit Margin
- Monthly Sales & Profit Trend
- State-wise Profit Analysis

### Customer Intelligence
- Customer KPIs
- Top Customers by Sales
- Customer Segment Analysis
- Regional Customer Distribution

### Product Intelligence
- Best & Least Performing Categories
- Top Selling Sub-Categories
- Sales vs Profit Analysis
- Product Performance Comparison

### Profit Optimization
- Profit Margin Analysis
- Profit Loss Estimation
- Risk Distribution
- Regional Profitability Heatmap
- Profit Improvement Analysis

---

## Key Features

- Interactive slicers and filters
- KPI Cards
- Dynamic DAX Measures
- Drill-down Analysis
- Geographic Map Visualization
- Scatter Plot Analysis
- Heatmap Visualization
- Business-focused Dashboard Design

---

## Key Insights

- Technology is the highest-performing category in terms of sales and profit.
- Furniture contributes the lowest overall profitability.
- Higher discounts negatively impact profit margins.
- Customer purchasing behavior differs across regions and market segments.
- Profit optimization opportunities can be identified through discount and loss analysis.
